# Administrator and support guide

Edition 1.1. This describes administration inside one owner's synthetic alpha. It does not provision real government organisations or independently authenticated staff.

## Institution and memberships

Use Alex's test administrator profile. Inspect institution status before testing a protected workflow. For Cedar onboarding, Maya submits the application; Alex returns, rejects or approves with the required reason. A synthetic approval is not legal verification.

In Institution & team, revoke/restore non-administrator synthetic memberships. A revoked profile fails protected use; historical attribution remains. The administrator profile is protected by the alpha interface. For real institutional administration, use the proposed R3 identity design only after implementation and independent qualification.

## Grant administration

Distinguish legacy dataset/study grants, decided by the custodian Sam, from workbench audio/support access, decided by Alex. Scope, purpose and expiry are separate inputs. A playback grant does not imply inference/export. Uploaded live-connection inference requires both inference and export permission.

Support receives only authorised aggregate diagnostics under an approved, unexpired grant. Do not grant another owner's data through a test role. Dummy organisation invitations and service identities neither send invitations nor create working external credentials.

## Safety and evidence

Noor controls safety concerns/holds. Administration is not authority to overwrite an issued report or approve one's own evidence. Correction/supersession and withdrawal preserve history. Investigate hash mismatches and restrict affected operations while preserving the record.

## Secrets and checkpoints

Runtime signing and checkpoint secrets are infrastructure settings, not form data or repository files. Live connector credentials are entered only in the protected connector field and stored encrypted. A changed endpoint must not inherit a secret silently.

As Alex, capture and verify an encrypted checkpoint with no active hold, inspect its revision/counts and retain the encrypted archive through the authorised operator process. Five checkpoints is the current bounded policy; no UI/API deletion or rotation endpoint exists, and simultaneous creations have a documented cap limitation. Independent key escrow is not configured by this application.

Only the isolated restore tool restores data, into an empty local destination. Verification is not live restoration. Production recovery/cutover needs its own approved environment and rehearsal.

## Handover and operational ownership

Give a successor the repository revision, current docs, synthetic training scenario, open issues and operating responsibilities. Never copy credentials into the handover. Hosting, retention, independent escrow, incident escalation and real identity ownership must be assigned to actual institutional people before production acceptance.
