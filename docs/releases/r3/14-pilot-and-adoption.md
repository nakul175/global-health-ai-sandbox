# Institutional pilot and adoption plan

Status: proposed customer validation plan. Initial scenario: a health programme evaluates three synthetic cough-screening supplier submissions and prepares a district pilot decision. It is an evaluation-workflow demonstration, not a TB diagnostic claim.

## Partner and ownership

Choose one programme with an accountable director, evaluator, finance representative, technical integration contact and independent review authority. Confirm the decision the pilot will inform, the actual committee format and the available context/cost data. Assign organisation administrators and handover deputies. No partner, ministry, regulator or procurement endorsement is assumed.

## Pilot steps and deliverables

| Step | Work | Acceptance deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Define | Agree intended decision, success measures, roles, data class and limits | Signed-off pilot brief held in the institution's process |
| 2. Configure | Institution users, context, templates, reference costs and quotas | Completed context profile with sources and unknowns |
| 3. Assess | Invite/record suppliers, freeze protocol, execute comparable scenarios | Reproducible results and evidence gaps |
| 4. Budget | Compare baseline and scenarios, identify capacity/funding gaps | Reviewed cost and funding schedule |
| 5. Decide | Independent review and committee pack | Recorded decision, conditions and next evidence required |
| 6. Handover | Train successor users, export records, rehearse support | Institution can retrieve/reproduce its decision trail |

## Training

Programme officers: create context, understand evidence classes and interpret uncertainty. Evaluators: protocols, rights, failed runs and immutable evidence. Finance: sources, currency, sensitivity and funding gaps. Administrators: membership, support grants and handover. Suppliers: submission format, versioning and released feedback. Use the same synthetic fixtures across training and acceptance so expected results are explainable.

## Adoption measures

Track completed evaluations and actual decision use rather than logins alone. Record analyst time, manual corrections, support requests, time from frozen protocol to committee pack, percentage of cost lines with sources, funding gaps identified, independently reproduced runs and whether a successor can continue the work. Use baseline task observations before asserting time or cost savings.

## Commercial readiness

Before a paid institutional deployment: agree scope, hosting/data ownership, responsibility for evaluation methodology, support/service levels, transparent usage limits, training, supplier neutrality and exit rights. Pricing is a separate decision. Configurable procurement templates require local review and do not constitute legal advice or an automatic award recommendation.

## Boundaries for clinical pilots

A clinical pilot needs approved evidence-generation protocol, local permissions, appropriate reference standard, representative data, statistical plan, safety oversight and a verified deployment profile. Its clinical decisions remain with authorised health professionals. Technical synthetic testing can precede those decisions and cannot replace them.
